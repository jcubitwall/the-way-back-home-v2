‹ The Way Back Home
Makes a 16-page pocket booklet (4¼ × 5½ in) from one 11 × 17 sheet.
  1. Print double-sided, “flip on short edge”.
  2. Lay it with pages 2 and 3 facing up (right-way-up pictures along the top).
  3. Fold the right half over the left. Fold the bottom half up. Fold the right half over the left again.
  4. Turn it over so the cover faces you. Staple the left fold, then cut along the bottom and right folded edges.
